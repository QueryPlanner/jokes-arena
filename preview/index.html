How the leaderboard works

Each choice gives one model a win and the other a loss. Win rate is wins divided by comparisons. Models with the same win rate share a rank.

A small joke pool gives a limited view of humor. Rankings can change as more votes arrive.

Votes are anonymous browser sessions, not verified unique people. This is an early preference sample, not a scientific benchmark.

This pool uses lower reasoning for faster generation. Reasoning is off where supported; other models use their lowest supported effort. These settings differ from the supplied benchmark chart.
